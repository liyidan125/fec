I. Organization and Management System 一、组织与管理体系

Q1. Data Team Setup and Responsibility Boundaries 数据团队设置与职责边界
Please clarify whether FEC has established a dedicated data team. If so, detail its organizational positioning, reporting lines, and responsibility boundaries (e.g., does it cover data governance, platform construction, data services, and business support?), and specify who leads the team.
请说明 FEC 是否已设立专门的数据团队。如有，请进一步说明其组织定位、汇报关系及职责边界（是否涵盖数据治理、数据平台建设、数据服务与业务支持等），并由哪位负责人统筹管理。
🎯 Focus / 关注要点： Independent establishment; reporting lines to IT/Business; team size and roles. / 团队是否独立建制；与 IT、业务部门的汇报关系；人员规模与角色构成。
Q2. Data Ownership Mechanism and Requirement Handling 数据属主机制与需求受理
How are Data Owners set up for various business lines and products—are they decentralized by product line or centralized under a unified data team? Is data shared and interconnected across teams? How does the daily workflow for proposing, evaluating, and delivering data requirements operate?
请说明各业务线及产品的数据属主（Data Owner）如何设置——是按产品条线分散归属，还是由统一的数据团队集中归口？各团队之间的数据是否实现共享与互通？日常数据需求的提出、评估与交付流程如何运转？
🎯 Focus / 关注要点： Ownership division principles; cross-departmental data sharing; standardized workflow and response time. / 属主划分原则；跨部门数据共享程度；需求受理的标准化流程与平均响应时效。
Q3. Main Obstacles in Advancing Data Initiatives 数据工作推进的主要阻力
What are the main obstacles and bottlenecks in advancing data initiatives currently? Are they budget constraints, lack of professional talent, cross-departmental collaboration issues, or the business side's trust and willingness to use data applications?
当前数据工作推进过程中，最主要的阻力与瓶颈集中在哪些方面？是预算投入、专业人才、跨部门协同，还是业务侧对数据应用的信任程度与使用意愿？
🎯 Focus / 关注要点： Ranking and causes of core bottlenecks; historical resolution attempts; critical blockers for business. / 核心瓶颈的排序与成因；历史尝试解决的方式与效果；是否已形成制约业务的关键卡点。

II. Platform Architecture and Technology Stack 二、平台架构与技术体系

Q4. Overall Data Platform Architecture and Tech Stack 数据平台整体架构与技术栈
Please outline the overall architecture and technology stack of FEC's current data platform, including deployment models (public, private, or hybrid cloud), core components, and key selection criteria.
请概述 FEC 当前数据平台的整体架构与技术栈，包括部署形态（公有云、私有云或混合云）、核心组件构成及关键选型依据。
🎯 Focus / 关注要点： Cloud deployment model and provider; core components; architecture scalability. / 云部署模式及服务商；存储、计算、调度等核心组件；架构扩展性。
Q5. Business Data Integration and Management Scale 业务数据对接与纳管规模
How is data from core business systems (like deposits and loans) integrated into the data platform (integration methods, frequency, and pipelines)? What is the total scale and growth trend of the data currently managed by the platform?
请说明存款、贷款等核心业务系统的数据如何对接并接入数据平台（集成方式、频率及链路），以及目前平台纳管数据的总体规模与增长情况。
🎯 Focus / 关注要点： Integration completeness and timeliness; total volume and daily increments; unmanaged system silos. / 数据接入完整性与时效；数据总量与日增量；孤岛系统。
Q6. Data Governance Platform and Management Mechanism 数据治理平台与管理机制
Has FEC established data governance platforms and mechanisms such as data asset management, metadata management, and data access authorization? Please evaluate their coverage and maturity.
请说明 FEC 是否已建立数据资产管理、元数据管理及数据权限与授权等数据治理平台与机制，并评估其覆盖范围与成熟度。
🎯 Focus / 关注要点： Asset catalog and metadata capabilities; data lineage; access control and security compliance. / 资产目录与元数据管理；数据血缘；权限管控与合规。
Q7. Data Quality and Monitoring System 数据质量与数据监控体系
What is the current state of FEC's data quality management and monitoring? Does it have complete quality rule definitions, monitoring and alerting mechanisms, and a closed-loop issue resolution process?
请说明 FEC 在数据质量管理与数据监控方面的现状——是否具备完整的质量规则定义、监控告警机制及问题闭环处置流程。
🎯 Focus / 关注要点： Quality rule coverage; issue discovery methods; responsible parties and closed-loop timelines. / 质量规则覆盖度；问题发现方式；修复责任主体与闭环时效。

🎯 Focus / 关注要点： Business scenarios and effects; platform technical capabilities; deployment efficiency. / 落地业务场景与效果；平台技术能力；上线效率。

请说明 FEC 是否已部署面向业务侧自助使用的数据分析与 BI 平台，及其用户覆盖范围与活跃使用情况。
🎯 Focus / 关注要点： Business self-service analysis capabilities; dashboard activity levels; requirement backlog status. / 业务自助分析能力；看板活跃度；需求积压情况。

面向未来，FEC 期望构建怎样的数据平台、数据管理体系及数据协作模式？在数据层面，业务与技术的中长期规划与重点投入方向是什么？
🎯 Focus / 关注要点： Benchmark platforms and expectations; top prioritized problems; construction priorities and resources for the next 1-3 years. / 对标平台与能力期望；最希望优先解决的 Top 问题；未来 1–3 年的建设优先级与投入。
